ABOUT US

We're a small team that treats your site like it's the only one we're building.

"Yezhuththu" is the Tamil word for script — the letters that carry a message from one person to another. That's what we think a website, a campaign, or a search result should do: carry your work to the person looking for it.

HOW WE WORK

A few things we hold ourselves to.

  • One point of contact

    You talk to the person actually building your project, not an account manager relaying messages.

  • Plain answers on cost and time

    You get a scope and a timeline before work starts, and you hear from us if either one changes.

  • Built to be handed over

    Your site and its content are yours — documented and easy for you or another team to pick up later.

  • Measured, not just launched

    We check what's working after launch — traffic, rankings, conversions — and adjust rather than walk away.
